About Us

The studio
behind the craft

We believe great design doesn't just look beautiful — it cuts through the noise.

CutFusion Studio is a collective of senior motion designers, art directors, and visual strategists with 12+ years of combined expertise. We partner with world-class brands, agencies, and forward-thinking startups.

Based in New York with a global reach, our studio blends storytelling with surgical precision — choreographing title sequences, sculpting 3D brand worlds, and crafting visuals that stop people cold.

Our arsenal: After Effects, Cinema 4D, Figma, Blender, and the full Adobe suite. But beyond tools, we bring a relentless understanding of timing, rhythm, and emotional impact to every single frame.
